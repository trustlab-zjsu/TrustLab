## Duplicate and status decisions

- The three Scholar entries “Smart contract vulnerability detection technique: A survey”, “智能合约安全漏洞检测技术研究综述”, and “A survey of smart contract security vulnerability detection techniques” all refer to Journal of Software 33(8):3059–3085. They produce one journal entry with the publisher's English title and all six authors.
- “Empirical review of smart contract and defi security: Vulnerability detection and automated repair” (arXiv:2309.02391) is the earlier version of the ESWA 2025 comprehensive review. Only the published review appears in the accepted/published catalogue.
- The arXiv entry for the graph-feature/expert-pattern paper is represented by its verified IJCAI 2021 publication, not as a second preprint.
- The Scholar entry titled “International Joint Conferences on Artificial Intelligence Organization” has malformed bibliographic metadata and no identifiable independent paper. It is not presented as an additional publication.
- “DiT-SGCR: Directed Temporal Structural Representation with Global-Cluster Awareness for Ethereum Malicious Account Detection” remains an arXiv preprint (2506.20123) in the available records. It is not added as an accepted paper.
- The existing HalluProp and trajectory-forecasting preprints stay in the Preprints tab. No acceptance is inferred from their presence on Scholar.
- TKDE is displayed as 2023 (volume 35, issue 2), iQuery as 2023 (volume 20, issue 6), and the Journal of Software survey as 2022 (volume 33, issue 8). These are final issue years; Scholar also contains earlier online-publication years.
- FreeWavm is published in PACMSE 2(ISSTA), DOI 10.1145/3728877. It appears once under Conference Papers, consistent with the author's ISSTA 2025 research-paper listing and the website's existing ISSTA venue convention.
- SwFuzz is the authors' rendered name for the publisher's mathematical title `S$w$Fuzz`; Scholar/IEEE indexing sometimes flattens it to `SFuzz`.
